## What this identity means

We research and develop evidence-centered systems across AI, scientific computing, decision controls, and accountable infrastructure. Our work asks what a system knows, what it can demonstrate, where its uncertainties remain, and whether it should commit to an action.

## Distinct research and engineering lines

- **Neuro-reactive Multimodal Metacognition (NMM)** — a published proposed closed-loop architecture linking AI self-monitoring to uncertain human-state inference; full empirical validation remains open.
- **Artificial Machine Understanding (AMU), MITHL, MRL, and CCG** — related but individually bounded research constructs; terminology should not collapse separate historical development.
- **Second Thought** — implementation and experimental evaluation of machine-side pre-commit control and auditable interventions, without claims of general accuracy superiority.
- **ASTRID / Roman microlensing** — separate computational astronomy work; public quantitative results need their reproducible scientific artifacts.
- **Glass Substrate Protocol (GSP)** — distinct accountability, audit, and governance work.
